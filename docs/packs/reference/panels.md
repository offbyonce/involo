## PanelItemKind

What a panel item is, which decides how it is drawn.

| Value | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `toggle_switch` | A lever switch flipped between its positions, such as the battery switch; may be guarded. |
| `selector` | A rotary knob turned to one of its named positions, drawn on a dial (`angles`). |
| `push_button` | A button pressed and released; may be guarded. |
| `light` | An annunciator light in one of the light roles (`color`). |
| `gauge` | A round dial with a pointer, such as a pressure or quantity gauge. |
| `readout` | A small digital window showing a value, such as a frequency or a meter display; drawn at its `width` and `height`. |
| `display_unit` | A screen, such as a flight or engine display; drawn at its `width` and `height`. |
| `lever` | A lever moved along a slot, such as the start, speedbrake, gear and flap levers. |
| `handle` | A handle that is pulled, such as the parking brake and the fire handles. |
| `push_pull_knob` | A rotary knob that is also pushed in or pulled out. Two positions: pushed in first, pulled out second (format 6). |
| `legend_pushbutton` | A pushbutton with one or two legends that light (`legends`), set to one of its `positions` (format 6). |
| `rocker` | A two-way switch that tips about its middle. Two positions: the top end pressed first, the bottom end second (format 6). |
| `keypad` | A block of keys: its `positions` are the keys, row by row, `columns` to a row (format 6). A step presses one key per target. |
