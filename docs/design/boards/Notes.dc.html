Light meanings
LOW
PRESSURE
Amber when lit: that pump's output pressure is low.
NormallyOut, with the pump switched on and fuel in its tank.
When it comes onThe pump is switched off, has failed, or its tank is empty.
What to doCheck the pump switch is on. If it is and the light stays on, check that tank's fuel quantity.
