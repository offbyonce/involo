PERF INIT · page 10 of 15

Fuel, weight, reserves and cost index

Do

Press INIT REF, then PERF INIT. Enter the plan fuel at 2L, ZFW at 3L, reserves at 4L and the cost index at 5L.

Check

GW at 1L fills in from fuel plus ZFW. It should be near the planned takeoff weight (138.5).

Note

Weights and fuel in thousands of LB, rounded up to the next tenth. The sensed fuel at 2L (SENS) is what the tanks hold.
