Privacy

Involo keeps everything on this device. Here’s what that means.

What’s stored

  • Your progress on each flight: ticks, skips, timers, briefings and where you are.
  • Your settings, including the SimBrief username or pilot ID you typed.
  • The flight plan you imported, in short form: route, numbers and weather, with no names or other personal fields.
  • Your flight log and practice results.
  • The packs you installed.

Nothing else that could identify you.

Where it’s kept

In this browser, on this device only. There’s no account and nothing is copied anywhere else. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes all of it.

What’s sent

Nothing is sent to us: no analytics, no tracking and no cookies.

When you import a plan, your browser asks SimBrief for it directly, with the username you typed. Other than that, Involo connects only to its own site, to load the app, its updates and the packs you add.

Report a problem with a step opens GitHub’s issue form in a new tab. Nothing is sent unless you submit it there.

Where the app comes from

Involo is served by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Keep a copy

Because everything lives in this browser, save a backup file from time to time: Settings › Back up and restore.
