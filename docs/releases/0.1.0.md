**Involo 0.1.0 is the first public beta.** Open it at **https://offbyonce.github.io/involo/**.
It installs from your browser, works offline, and replaces the single-file 3.0 checklist for the
iFly 737 MAX 8, keeping every one of its steps. The desktop app for Windows and macOS is attached
below.

**For simulator use only.** Never use these checklists for real-world flight.

## What's in it

- **The iFly 737 MAX 8 pack:** 65 checklists and 527 steps across five stages, from cold and dark
  or a turnaround, with the exterior inspection and a top view of the airplane.
- **List or Step by step,** Full or Essentials (and Essentials of your own), and step details:
  what to check, why, how, and where each control is on its panel.
- **Your flight from SimBrief,** or from a saved OFP file offline, filling the steps and the CDU
  walkthroughs that need its values.
- **Decision steps, step timers, briefings,** an Emergency reference, Practice, the Glossary and
  References.
- **A flight log** on your device, and **Back up and restore** of everything as one file. A
  restore also reads a file exported by the 3.0 checklist, so you can carry on from 3.0.
- **Report a problem with a step** from any step's menu.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/offbyonce/involo/blob/v0.1.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Your data

Everything stays in your browser on this device: nothing is sent to the project, and SimBrief is
contacted straight from your browser, only when you import a plan. Clearing the site's data
removes it, so save a backup now and then (Settings › Back up and restore).
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/offbyonce/involo/blob/v0.1.0/SECURITY.md) lists exactly what is
stored and sent.

## The desktop app

The same app, keeping your progress as files on your computer:

- **Windows (64-bit):** `Involo_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe`
- **macOS, Apple silicon:** `Involo_0.1.0_aarch64.dmg`
- **macOS, Intel:** `Involo_0.1.0_x64.dmg`

Check your download against `involo-desktop-SHA256SUMS.txt`. The builds aren't signed yet, so
your system warns the first time: on Windows choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**; on macOS
Control-click Involo in Applications and choose **Open** (or **System Settings › Privacy &
Security › Open Anyway**). The [README](https://github.com/offbyonce/involo/blob/v0.1.0/README.md#the-desktop-app)
has the details. A backup file moves your progress between the web app and the desktop app.

## Attached

- **The packs** (`*.involo-pack`) and the catalog (`index.json`), the same files the site
  installs from.
- **The desktop app's installers,** with `involo-desktop-SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- **`involo-pack`,** the command for pack writers, for Windows, macOS (Apple silicon and Intel)
  and Linux, with `involo-pack-SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Found a problem? Use Report a problem with a step in the app, or open an issue.
